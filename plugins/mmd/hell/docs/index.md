# Feld-Hell

Feld-Hell is a Multi-Mode Decoder plugin. It prints Hellschreiber on a tape as it is heard, line after line, the newest line at the bottom. Each line is printed twice, one copy above the other, as Hell's own machines printed it: a print that slants out of one copy is whole in the other. Nothing is decoded into letters; you read the print.

Once the plugin is installed, pick **Feld-Hell** in the decoder's **Mode** menu. How the decoder itself works (its waterfall, Input Device, Rig Control and the Frequency menu) is in the GridTracker2 User Guide, under *The Multi-Mode Decoder*.

## Reading a Signal

* **Submode** (the row under the decoder's second line): **Feld-Hell**, **Feld-Hell X5** or **Feld-Hell X9**.
* The strongest Hell signal is found by itself, shown by cyan dashed lines on the waterfall. Click another signal on the waterfall to read it instead (orange lines), and click inside its band to go back to finding the strongest. The status shows where it is read and how strong.
* **Trim:** click **−** or **+** to straighten a print that slants: raise it if the print runs downhill to the right, lower it if uphill. Click the value to set it back to 0.
* A Feld Hell RSID clicked on the waterfall switches the decoder to Feld-Hell, its transmission printed from its start.
* **Log QSO** on the decoder's second line (with **Log QSOs By Hand** on in GridTracker2's Settings → Logging) opens the Log QSO window with the frequency of the signal read and the time it was found.

## Frequencies

The Frequency menu lists the Feld Hell Club's frequencies from 160 to 6 meters, and a 60 meter and a 2 meter frequency. **Set Mode** sets the rig to USB.

## Settings

In the decoder window's **Settings** → **Decoders**, under **Feld-Hell**:

* **Input Device** and **VFO:** as every mode has them. A new install starts on CW's Input Device.
* **Set Frequency** and **Set Mode:** what picking a frequency in the Frequency menu sends the rig. Shown with **Rig Control** on. Each is on unless you turn it off.

The decoder's **Test Signals** plays a Feld-Hell recording.
