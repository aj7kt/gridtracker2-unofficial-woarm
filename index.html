GridTracker2 Unofficial WoARM: download files

Files that the unofficial Windows on ARM builds of GridTracker2 download while they run, hosted here so those builds don't use the GridTracker.org servers. Not made or supported by GridTracker.org or the GridTracker2 team. The installers themselves are on the repository's Releases page.

  • dbs/: callsign and park reference data, copied from GridTracker.org's server, which builds it from the country files (cty.dat), Club Log, the FCC licence database (ULS), Canada's amateur callsign database, and Parks on the Air. Parks On The Air® is a registered trademark of Parks On The Air, LLC. Club Log © Michael Wells G7VJR.
  • plugins/mmd/: the Multi-Mode Decoder's decoder plugins, built from the GridTracker2 source (BSD 3-Clause) and signed with this project's own key: MCowBQYDK2VwAyEAhPceOF/fPIWg9TiZvJG/iVcwVxSSB3mybNzbKoMqWms=. The official GridTracker2 doesn't load them.

The official GridTracker2 is at gridtracker.org.
