BirdWatch satellite data: sources and licenses
================================================

modes.json, satellites.json, transmitters.json
  From SatNOGS DB (https://db.satnogs.org), developed, hosted and maintained by
  Libre Space Foundation (https://libre.space), with data contributed by its volunteers.
  Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license
  (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  The data is shipped as SatNOGS DB's API returns it; only its line endings are changed.

dailytle.txt
  AMSAT's daily element sets (TLE) of the amateur satellites, from AMSAT
  (The Radio Amateur Satellite Corporation, https://www.amsat.org): https://www.amsat.org/tle/current/

amsat-active-frequencies.json, amsat-all-frequencies.json
